JobSearchAgent

A personal job search assistant run by Miguel Chavez for his own use.

JobSearchAgent runs on its owner's own computer. Once a week it collects public job postings from employer career sites, checks that each posting is still open, drafts tailored resume suggestions for its owner to review, and sends its owner a single notification email when the weekly review is ready.

Use of Google data

The app requests one Google permission: send email (gmail.send). It uses it only to send the weekly notification from job.search.agent.usa@gmail.com to its owner's own inbox. It does not read, search, label or delete any email. See the privacy policy.

This is not a public service. It is not offered to other users.
