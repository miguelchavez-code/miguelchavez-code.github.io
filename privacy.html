Privacy policy

JobSearchAgent · Last updated October 16, 2026

JobSearchAgent is a personal tool used only by its owner, Miguel Chavez. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data the app accesses

The app requests a single Google OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send, which allows it to send email. It does not request access to read, search, modify or delete email, contacts, calendars, files or any other Google data.

How the data is used

The app sends one notification email per weekly run, from job.search.agent.usa@gmail.com to its owner's own address, saying that the weekly job review is ready. The email contains only job titles, employer names and a link to a review page that runs on the owner's computer.

Storage

The OAuth token that lets the app send email is stored only on the owner's computer. No Google data is stored on any server, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or for training models.

Limited Use

JobSearchAgent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
